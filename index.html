Infografis: Discussing Life, Events, and Hypothetical Situations

1 · DISCUSSING LIFEBerbagi pengalaman dan kehidupan.I’ve lived in Malang for five years.
2 · DISCUSSING EVENTSMenyampaikan pendapat tentang peristiwa.In my opinion, working from home is more flexible.
3 · HYPOTHETICALMembayangkan situasi yang tidak nyata.If I won the lottery, I would travel the world.

Ungkapan berpendapat

In my opinionI believeI agreeI see it differently

Sampaikan alasan dengan because: I agree, because it saves travel time.

Pola Kalimat Kondisional

Tipe 1: kondisi yang mungkin terjadi di masa depanIf + simple present, subject + will + V1
If I study hard, I will pass the exam.
Tipe 2: situasi hipotesis, khayalan/situasi mustahil di masa sekarangIf + past simple, subject + would + V1
If it weren't raining , I would go to library
Tipe 2: memakai wereIf I were you, I would accept the job
Tipe 3: masa lalu/penyesalanIf + had + V3, would have + V3
If I had studied, I would have passed.
